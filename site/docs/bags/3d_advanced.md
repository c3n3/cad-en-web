# 3D Advanced

Wrap sketches and textures onto faces, and bring in other models. These tools are on the **Features** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Wrap](../tools/wrap.md) | Wrap a sketch's surfaces onto a curved face, raised or cut. |
| [Texture](../tools/texture.md) | Give faces a surface texture from a pattern or image. |
| [Import Model](../tools/import_model.md) | Bring another model into this one whole, placed by constraints. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its demonstration and page.
