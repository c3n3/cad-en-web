# 3D basics

Turn sketches into solid bodies and shape their edges. These tools are on the **Features** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Extrude](../tools/extrude.md) | Push sketch surfaces into a solid. |
| [Cut Extrude](../tools/cut_extrude.md) | Cut a sketch's shape out of bodies. |
| [Fillet](../tools/fillet.md) | Round body edges. |
| [Chamfer](../tools/chamfer.md) | Bevel body edges. |
| [Swept Extrude](../tools/swept_extrude.md) | Sweep sketch surfaces along a path. |
| [Swept Cut](../tools/swept_cut.md) | Cut by sweeping a shape along a path. |
| [Rotate Extrude](../tools/rotate_extrude.md) | Revolve sketch surfaces about an axis. |
| [Rotate Cut](../tools/rotate_cut.md) | Cut by revolving a shape about an axis. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its demonstration and page.
