# Mechanical (assemblies)

Constraints that link how models move, on the **Assembly** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Geared](../tools/mate_geared.md) | Turn two models together at a fixed ratio, like gears in mesh. |
| [Rack and Pinion](../tools/mate_rack.md) | Turning a gear moves a rack along, and sliding the rack turns the gear. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its page.
