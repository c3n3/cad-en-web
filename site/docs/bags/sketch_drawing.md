# Sketch drawing

Draw the curves and shapes a sketch is made of. These tools are on the **Sketch** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Point](../tools/point.md) | Place points. |
| [Line](../tools/line.md) | Draw lines, chained end to end. |
| [Circle](../tools/circle.md) | A circle from its centre and a point on it. |
| [Arc](../tools/arc.md) | An arc from its centre, start and end. |
| [Ellipse](../tools/ellipse.md) | An axis-aligned ellipse from its centre, width and height. |
| [Rectangle](../tools/rectangle.md) | A rectangle from two corners, or from its centre. |
| [Slot](../tools/slot.md) | A slot from its two centres and its radius. |
| [Polygon](../tools/polygon.md) | A regular polygon from its centre and a corner. |
| [Spline](../tools/spline.md) | A smooth curve through the points you click. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its demonstration and page.
