# Sketch Advanced

Larger drawing tools that build several items at once. These tools are on the **Sketch** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Wall](../tools/wall.md) | A wall of even thickness following a construction centerline, with mitred corners and square ends. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its demonstration and page.
