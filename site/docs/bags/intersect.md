# Combine

Make new bodies out of the ones you have: join them into one, or split them where they overlap. These tools are on the **Features** tab of the ribbon.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Join](../tools/join.md) | Unite bodies that overlap or share a face into one body. |
| [Intersect](../tools/intersect.md) | Split bodies into their overlapping and separate pieces. |

Right-click any tool button and choose **Help** to see its demonstration and page.
