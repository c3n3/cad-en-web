# Release notes

- Joined select: a third choice on the Select button. One click on a sketch curve selects every curve joined to it end to end, at sharp corners as well as smooth ones
- Join Points (Sketch basics): joins the points of the items you pick that lie within a tolerance of each other (0.1 mm by default), closing the small gaps in an outline so it becomes a surface
- Dragging from a sketch surface that isn't selected now draws a selection box; click the surface first to drag the whole shape
- The sketch Rotate tool shows its copies as a preview while you fill in the form, as Mirror does
- The Rotate Feature and Mirror Feature help videos now show the axis and the plane being picked; new videos for Join Points and Joined select
