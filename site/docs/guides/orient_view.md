# Orienting the view

Turn the view to look straight at a face, or pick a side to look from.

## Look straight at a face
1. Select a flat face: a flat face of a body, a sketch's surface, or a plane.
2. Press **N**, or click the cube button at the top of the 3D view.

The view turns to look square at the face and zooms so the whole face is in view, centred. What was up on screen stays as close to up as it can. Curved faces aren't supported; with one selected (or an edge, a line or a point), the status bar says so and the view doesn't move.

## Pick a side to look from
1. Select nothing (press Escape to clear the selection).
2. Press **N**, or click the cube button.

A see-through cube appears round the model, like the navigation cube in the corner but without its axes. Click one of its faces, edges or corners to look from there, as the corner cube does. You can still pan and turn the view while the cube is up; it stays until you click it. Press Escape to put it away without changing the view.

## Tips
- The cube button is always at the top of the 3D view. While you edit a sketch it sits beside the grid button.
- N can be changed under the keyboard shortcuts (the command is **Orient View**).
